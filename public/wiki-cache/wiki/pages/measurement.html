01Overview

This project aims to utilize LATTE and HybriDock-Pep to solve real-world problems, like malaria diagnostic inaccuracies. Through this, we developed a new malaria diagnostic using the LOCKR sequence created by our software. In order to verify our diagnostic's efficacy, and, by proxy, the accuracy of our software, we had to ensure that the test can work in several different conditions, specifically those in the regions most affected by the HRP2 deletions in Plasmodium falciparum. The previously mentioned being the leading cause of the false negatives in current malaria tests. To replicate conditions in such regions, we built a humidity and temperature control chamber.
